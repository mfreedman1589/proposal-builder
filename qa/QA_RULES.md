# QA rules — what a tester can check from the app and its output

Each rule is client- or rep-facing and observable without reading code. The source names
the section of `CLAUDE.md` (or `DECISIONS.md`) that owns it — update this file in the same
commit as any change to one of these rules.

## Build a proposal — media plan and money

| # | Rule | How to observe | Source |

| P2 | **Broadcast lines are always gross** — never grossed up again. | An imported Wide Orbit line's cost equals the schedule's gross cost with the gross-up on or off. | CLAUDE.md › Broadcast and Wide Orbit › "Broadcast lines are always gross…" |
| P3 | **An imported Wide Orbit schedule replaces the rate-card broadcast line** — never a second one. | P4 | **Wide Orbit impressions are raw on the plan** — never in thousands. | Planner exports print 1,831.6; the plan shows 1,831,600. | CLAUDE.md › Broadcast and Wide Orbit › "…the units are the trap." |
| P5 | **A flat fee is never multiplied by the month count.** | A Flat Fee row's full-flight cost equals its stated amount. | CLAUDE.md › The media plan and the form › "A media-plan row can be Rate or Flat Fee…" |
| P6 | **Budget doesn't prorate for partial months.** A Monthly-breakout row's monthly cost is the same in a partial first/last month as in a full one. | Flight starting mid-month: no line scaled by a day fraction. | BACKLOG.md › Smaller / carry-over › "Day-prorating…out of scope" |
| P7 | **Changing the flight after a plan is priced holds Generate until the rep confirms the new totals** (one tick, "I've checked the plan totals for the new flight", per flight; no dismiss step). A Monthly plan keeps its monthly budget and its total follows the months; a Full-Flight plan keeps its total spend, spread across the new dates. | P8 | **A $0 media plan row never reaches the deck** (a freshly seeded rate row may sit at $0 on the grid until priced). | No $0 line in a generated plan. | CLAUDE.md › The media plan and the form › "A $0 media plan row is always wrong" |
| P9 | **One media plan option renders exactly like no options**; with 2–3, each option's name is appended to its plan title. | One option: plain title. Two: each plan slide titled with its option name. | CLAUDE.md › The media plan and the form › "A proposal carries 1–3 media plan options…" |
| P10 | **The plan title never repeats the client name** ("WAEPA WAEPA CTV Strategy"). | Plan slide title. | CLAUDE.md › The media plan and the form › "The plan slide title never echoes the client name twice" |
| P11 | **A plan row's Geo cell shows market names, not the Geography paragraph.** | Geo column reads "Washington, DC, Baltimore", not narrative text. | CLAUDE.md › The media plan and the form › "A plan row's Geo cell prefers the target markets…" |
| P12 | **An audience line combines at most 2 segments** (geography doesn't count; a CUSTOM segment counts as 1). Separate lines (OR) are unlimited, and there is no limit on custom audiences per campaign. | Audience finder in Build a proposal: AND two segments, then try a third AND — refused with a message. A drafted plan never shows a line built from 3+ segments; notes asking for 3 criteria produce a custom combo or 2 segments plus a review card offering the other pairs. An AND line with 2 segments offers no "often paired with". Several custom audiences on one plan raise no warning. | CLAUDE.md › The media plan and the form › "An audience line ANDs at most 2 segments…" |
| P15 | **Stated impression counts are priced as counts.** "500K CTV impressions" puts exactly 500,000 a month on the line (cost = count x CPM); a monthly figure on a monthly plan is never divided by the month count; "a retargeting pot with a similar number of impressions" matches the CTV total. | Good/better/best notes with stated counts: the plan's Impressions column shows the stated numbers. | CLAUDE.md › Drafting › "A stated count is priced as a count…" |
| P16 | **A multi-option plan lists media lines first** -- the primary market, then the markets in the order the notes named them -- **and retargeting and fees last**; the primary market's line is never smaller than a secondary one without a Generate check firing. | Plan slide row order; a plan where DC (primary) is smaller than Atlanta shows the check above Generate. | same |
| P17 | **Generate is held by a pre-Generate check, each with its own "generate anyway" tick**: an option's month over 1.5x the largest $ figure in the notes; a line at 50%+ of its avails the notes didn't ask for; fewer or more options than the notes asked for; an option that priced to $0 (never dropped silently); the primary market below a secondary; a flight starting before today. | The red messages above Generate and the disabled button until each is ticked. | same |
| P18 | **A flight given without a year is the next one coming** ("Jan-June" in November 2026 is Jan-Jun 2027), never one in the past. | The Flight row and the setup band after a draft. | same |
| P19 | **CTV is the only negotiable rate.** Every draft with CTV shows a "CTV rate" row: "$32.00 rate card." when the notes give no CPM, "$34.00 from the notes, above the $32.00 rate card." when they do -- editable either way, and the edit reaches every option's CTV lines. **Retargeting, sports and Audience Marketplace always price at the rate card**; a different rate in the notes shows a row ("Retargeting: notes say $6.00, rate card is $5.50. Priced at the rate card.") defaulting to the rate card. | Review panel after a draft; plan CPMs. | app.py `rate_review_items`, `fixed_rate_items`; tests/test_multi_option_draft.py |
| P14 | **Re-pricing never changes a Monthly option's monthly total** -- "Add all to plan", ticking an audience in D2, or a follow-up card's split re-divides the same budget; a 3-month $40,000 plan stays $13,333 a month. | Deck preview "Monthly totals" before and after "Add all to plan" or a split card on a Monthly option. | app.py `_allocate_group_rows`; tests/test_draft_followups.py |
| P13 | **Share of voice (% of avails) is per line and only where the line has real avails** — never a deck-wide figure, never 0% or ∞%; a real share under 1% reads "<1%" (QA-027). | With "show % of avails" on: matched lines show "(X% of avails)", unmatched lines show nothing. The draft review's reach note names each line's audience and market. | CLAUDE.md › The media plan and the form › "% of avails (share of voice) is per LINE…" |

## Build a proposal — sports lineup (2027)

Rates are net CPM, from `qa/ANSWER_KEY.md` "sports lineup". Dev and the public app read one rate card, which the nightly merge updates with this code (`setup_supabase.py sports`) -- until then dev prices three packages at last year's rate (Live Sports Select $50, Golf and NCAA Football Conferences $60) and Motorsports/Sports Select at the lineup rate. That gap alone is not a finding.
